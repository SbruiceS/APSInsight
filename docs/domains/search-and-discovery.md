## Quality indicators

- **completeness**: mean over records of (declared schema fields present and non-null) / (declared schema fields); 1.0 when the schema declares no fields
- **validity**: (records whose validation report has zero error-severity issues) / (records evaluated)
- **freshness_days**: mean and max of (now - updated_at) in days
- **provenance_coverage**: (records with a source reference or a documented transformation) / (records evaluated)

Absent attributes and explicit nulls both count as missing. With zero records every indicator is null rather than 0 or 1, so empty collections are never reported as perfect or failing.

Indicators describe structure and documentation, not scientific correctness: a complete, valid record can still hold a wrong measurement. Archived records are excluded. Freshness says nothing about whether old data is still representative.

## Operational behavior

Every change runs in an all-or-nothing transaction, increments the record version, appends provenance and history, updates indexes, emits matching notifications and appends a hash-chained audit event. Metrics: `apsi_operations_total{operation,domain="search-and-discovery",outcome}`.
